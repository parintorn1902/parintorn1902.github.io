Try the same interactions in every world

Story mode: scroll over the world or drag the journey slider. The camera travels continuously between the six stations. Click a station, then inspect it to see the matching project or profile information.

Play as Boop: move with WASD or arrow keys, jump with Space, and inspect with E. Drag the world to turn the camera. Phones have movement and jump buttons. Falling returns Boop to a safe spot.

Actual project screenshots and source links are included. The bottom switcher changes themes. Pause motion stops ambient animation and makes story travel immediate.

These are isolated theme prototypes. The main portfolio is still the previous Blender workshop while the visual direction is being selected.
